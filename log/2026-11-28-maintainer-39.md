## Why stage 17

Users of the tool asked for four changes to how `sort_consistency` places and judges its test actor, all needed to check one scene before it is settled:

- A walker on a raised walkway or a skywalk whose object also holds its rails or a roof gets no position: the actor box meets the object's size box. In a small scene, a deck 6 × 2 whose parts are a slab 0.2 high and two rails up to 1.2 gives 0 positions at `level` 2.2 between the rails, where a plain slab gives 224. `parts` tests the drawn part boxes instead.
- Vehicles, boats and trains move along lanes and turn with them; the grid over an area with one footprint cannot test them. `lanes` takes positions inside the lanes' pieces, at the surface height, with the actor's length along the piece.
- Results flip when an object moves by less than `step`. `edges` adds the positions where the actor touches each side of an examined object, so its closest approach moves with it.
- Accepted mismatches could only be left out of `ids`, which hides them from every later change. `allow` names objects or pairs accepted out of order and reports which entries were used.

`occluders` gets `--parts` for the same reason as the first. A check without the new parameters keeps the results of 0.16.0, so existing scenes do not change.

## Test data

`tests/fixtures/actor/` comes from the maintainer's reference implementation, extended for this stage: the reference's general code path, given `allow: []` so that nothing changes, gives the results of its stage 16 code on 40 random scenes of the stage 16 review in every state (585 results, all equal), under both engines. The scene's 19 checks in three states cover `parts` (0 positions on the walkway without it, 513 with it), `lanes` (a climbing ramp and the walkway's lane, an L-shaped road with a blocked corner, a diagonal lane under a viaduct, a lane of zero length that skips), `edges` (64 grid positions round the tower, 92 with contact positions), and `allow` (a pair, an object id with an unused entry, under both engines). The occluders on the walkway's lane find 0 positions without `--parts` and 160 with it. The invalid cases cover the types of the four parameters, `lanes` with `area`, `level` or `edges`, and unknown lanes and objects.
