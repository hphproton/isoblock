## Why stage 16

A user of the tool reported that an engine sorting one key per sprite draws a large scene in the wrong order in many places, and asked whether long objects had to be cut into shorter pieces. Measured with the reference implementation on that scene: most errors were between objects at different base heights (a deck over a lower building, a walkway sunk below the street), then next to an actor beside such objects; parts outside the footprint caused a few; no object was longer than the slices allow. Cutting objects would not have fixed it. Keys cannot order objects at several heights, so stage 16 adds the precomputed order that SPEC section 13.4 already listed among its fixes.

Measured on that scene with the reference, the order rule leaves out of order mostly objects that stand inside others (a carriage between the parapets of a deck, a cart under an awning), and marking the parts in front as sprites of their own leaves few, with small areas.

## Stage 16

- **The order rule** (SPEC 13.4): a static order of every sprite, computed from the needed orders of their pieces, ties and cycles broken by key; each actor drawn right after the last sprite below it (its slot). The key rule stays for engines that sort one number.
- **Parts marked `"sprite": "own"`** (SPEC 6, 13.4): an awning or a near parapet becomes sprites of its own, under both rules.
- **`sort_consistency`** with `engine` (`key` by default, `order`) and `pairs`, the objects whose pieces conflict; **`no_overlap`** with `parts` (SPEC 9.9).
- **`isoblock-runtime/4`**: `order` on every sprite (SPEC 13.7). The Godot adapter reads it, draws by the order rule and offers `actor_slot` (SPEC 13.8).
- **The object raster** keeps each object's full mask for the box of its outlines only (SPEC 14.1), so the editor opens a frame of 12,000 × 8,000 with 300 objects, which release 0.15.0 cannot (it asks for one frame-sized mask per object, about 29 GB here). The editor shows every valid scene and says when a check cannot be computed (SPEC 10).
- Known limits for the new rule (SPEC 18); the stage row and the order fixtures (SPEC 17); `ROADMAP.md`; review step 24 (`MAINTAINING.md`).
