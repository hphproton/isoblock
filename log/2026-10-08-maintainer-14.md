## Prototype

Everything was tried first with the maintainer's own Godot 4.7.1 driver (not in the repository; it uses only the stage 7 adapter's projection and face helpers) and measured with the maintainer's own checker:

| Frame | Differing pixels of 1,000,000 | Each object alone |
|---|---|---|
| `walk` default, `open`, `empty` | 2, 1, 2 | 0.41 px with clipped polygons (26 px with the old box clipping) |
| `yard` `closed` | 0 | 0.48 px |
| `court` `cleared` | 3 | 0.39 px |
| `court` `cleared`, six types instanced from white fake art colored through `modulate` | 3 | — |
| `court` default (the static mismatch of the box) | 153 | — |

- `clip_children` works in the Compatibility renderer: a 20 × 20 mask over a 90 × 90 polygon draws 400 pixels, all in the exact `modulate` color.
- Actor paths: 15 positions in 16 path points on `yard`, `garden` and `court` (`cleared`), each checked with the reference (free; wrong area 0 against every sprite; 7 positions on a sprite's key). Against the SVG of the scene with the actor added as an object: 1 to 3 differing pixels, the same as without the actor.
- Negative controls: the actor always on top gives up to 5,657 more pixels; actors before scene sprites on equal keys give 30, 33 and 231 more pixels on three tie positions (so `actorLimit` is 10, not the 100 of the whole frame); the instanced case without clipping gives 1,221, 2,255 and 3,654 more pixels at three positions where the actor stands between the slices of the bench and the counter. With the plain `court` frames the clipping made no difference, which is why the instanced case has an actor path.
- Editor (own probe, phone profile at 4x CPU slowdown): dragging on `walk` takes a median 400 ms per frame with its checks, 16.7 ms without `reachable`; `court` without its `sort_consistency` checks 16.7 ms. The criterion is reachable once the grid checks wait for the drop.
