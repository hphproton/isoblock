```
Stage 12: heights and cameras
```

Body:

```
A camera can be given as a view: elevation, direction and scale.
Objects can stand at a base height, so one object rests on another and a
flat object takes no space; zones and lanes have heights. A state can
hide parts of an object and frame regions. move, add and lock take a
height. Sort keys include the base height, and slices are weighted by
the camera.

The runtime file becomes isoblock-runtime/3. The Godot adapter reads it,
projects with its screen vectors and places actors at a height. The
editor draws both camera forms and objects at their heights, keeps the
base height in a drag and has a pos.h field.

Release 0.12.0.
```
