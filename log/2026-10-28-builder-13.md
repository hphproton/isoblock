## Done

- Object raster of SPEC 14.1. A part outline is the convex hull of its 8 projected corners, snapped to 1/256 px, filled by the top-left rule. Pixels take the last drawn part in the painter's order of the state. Full and visible masks are kept per object and per tag. `--grow` dilates a mask by the Chebyshev distance, up to 64 px.
- `export --target masks -o <dir>` writes `labels.png` (16-bit, file index, never grown), the cropped full and visible PNGs, the tag-group PNGs and `masks.json`. PNG encoding is in `src/cli` with `node:zlib`. `-o` is required. `--bbox-units`, `--bbox-order` and `--grow` are rejected on the targets they do not belong to. A scene of more than 65,535 objects is a usage error.
- `visibility` reports full and visible pixels, shares and coverage for a frame region, or the whole raster. An unknown region and a region the state hides are usage errors.
- `occluders` lists objects that hide an actor in a flat lane or a zone, by the painter's order, with the number of positions and the largest overlap. A grid of more than 1,000,000 points asks for a larger step.
- Check `visible_share`: exactly one of `ids` and `tag`, `min` from 0 to 1, optional `region`. Shares are rounded to 6 decimals. No examined object, and a hidden region, are `skip`. One raster per state serves every such check of a run. `compare` labels the row `visible_share <id> (% shown)` and prints a whole percent, halves up. In the editor the row waits for the drop, like `reachable` and `sort_consistency`.
