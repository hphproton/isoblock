## Done

- **SVG and block image** (`src/cli/svg.ts`): a polygon with an opacity below 1 is written as `fill-opacity` and `stroke-opacity`, both with that value. Opacity 1 omits the attributes. The group attribute `opacity` is not used. `render -o out.png` writes `openstrip`, which release 0.8.0 stopped on (`E_INTERNAL`, exit 70). Block images of `yard`, `garden`, `openstrip` and `states/yard.closed` match the fixtures of this release (channel difference at most 1).
- **`in_region`** (`measure` `"footprint"` or `"drawn"`, `maxOutside` 0 to 1, default 0): `"drawn"` measures the screen box of every part, the same box as `min_screen_size`. The share outside is `1 - area(box ∩ region) / area(box)`. A box with no area has share 0 when it lies inside the region (edges included) and 1 otherwise. An object fails when the share is over `maxOutside`, or when the strip rule fails. `threshold` stays 0. `outside` lists the shares of the failing objects, in object order, rounded to 6 decimals (`[]` when none fail). The footprint measure has no `outside` field. `maxOutside` without `measure: "drawn"` is a schema error. An incremental update still measures only the changed objects.
- **`min_screen_size`** with `measure: "height"`: `(highest part - lowest part) x pxPerUnit x verticalScale x screenWidth / frame.w`, rounded to 6 decimals. `"bounds"` stays the height of the screen box. A hidden target is `skip`.
- **`lane_apart`**: `lane`, `target` (`lane:<id>` or `zone:<id>`), optional `min` (default 0). Two lanes use the rectangle gap and the rectangle intersection, so the distance matches `clearance`. A zone is clipped to the lane rectangle (the area does not depend on the point order) and the distance is the edge distance, or 0 when a corner lies inside or two edges cross. Pass when the overlap is at most 1e-9 and the distance is at least `min`. `value` and `overlap` are rounded to 6 decimals. `ids` is `[lane, target id]`. Another lane shape, or a zone with fewer than 3 points, is `skip` and has no `overlap`. Two shapes that only touch pass at `min` 0. A state does not change the result, and moving an object does not re-run the check.
- **`compare`**: labels `in_region <region>[/<strip>] drawn (objects outside)`, `min_screen_size <target> height (px)` and `lane_apart <lane>×<target id> (<unit>)`. `lane_apart` cells use 2 decimals. `min_screen_size` cells stay whole pixels.
- **Editor**: the check panel already lists every result. On `measures`, the rows for the new measures and `lane_apart` equal `check --json` of the saved file, including `outside` and `overlap`.
- **States**: under `--state`, a hidden object leaves a drawn `in_region` list, and a hidden `min_screen_size` target, including `"height"`, is `skip`.
- `docs/AGENT_GUIDE.md`: the new parameters and `lane_apart`, and what a solver `conflict` means. `conflict` means this search found no layout. Objects that start inside a lane or on top of each other can make it miss a layout that meets every hard relation, and the conflict set can name more relations than are in the way. Place the objects roughly, or solve fewer with `--only`, and solve again. Do not apply the proposal of a `conflict`.
