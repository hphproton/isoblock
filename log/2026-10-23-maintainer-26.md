## Decisions

- **Camera, view form** (SPEC 5). A camera is either the axes form of stages 1 to 11 or a view form: `elevation`, `direction` (default 45) and `scale`, an orthographic camera. Both give the screen vectors `axisU`, `axisV` and `up`, and everything downstream uses them. `scale` is the length of a unit across the view, not `pxPerUnit`: in the view form the two ground axes have different screen lengths unless the direction is 45 plus a multiple of 90, so an axis length would be ambiguous. At direction 45 the view form equals an axes form, and the SPEC gives the conversion; the emulation users built (an `angleU` from the elevation and a `verticalScale`) is that conversion.
- **Base heights** (SPEC 6, 9.5). `pos` may have a third number. An object's **box** is its footprint from its base height to the base height plus its type's height. One rule decides where an object takes space: above a level and below a clear height. It drives `no_overlap` (boxes, so an object resting on another and a flat object no longer overlap), `lane_clear` (at the lane's level, with an optional `height`), `reachable` (on one level, with optional `level` and `height`), the actor positions of `sort_consistency` (with `level`) and the solver's constraint. `clearance`, `capacity`, `lane_apart` and relations stay on the ground plan. A flat object (height 0) now takes no space, which is what users asked for; no earlier fixture has a flat object in these checks.
- **Zones and lanes at heights.** A zone has one `h`; a lane has an `h` and may give each point its own height. Only flat lanes are checked by `lane_clear`; `lane_reaches` projects each end at its height. Zones are drawn in ascending height, all under the objects (a known limit in SPEC 18: a raised surface is drawn by the object that carries it).
- **Sort keys and slices** (SPEC 13.4). A key is twice the depth of the footprint's center at the base height (`+ 2·b`); an actor's key uses the height it stands on. This keeps ground scenes unchanged, orders actors under a high deck before it and objects on top after their support, but an actor on the far side of a wide raised object can still be drawn before the slice it stands on; `sort_consistency` with `level` measures it (the case `sort_deck` of `levels` shows it) and SPEC 18 says so. Slices are weighted by `|cu|` and `|cv|`, so that a sprite's footprint approaches `|cu|·w = |cv|·d`, the shape for which one key is right; with `|cu| = |cv|` they are those of stages 7 to 11.
- **States with parts and regions** (SPEC 9.5). `hideParts` (object id → part ids) and `hideRegions`. A hidden part is not drawn, occludes nothing, leaves the sprites and the generation box, while the object keeps its footprint and box for every footprint check. An object whose every part is hidden counts as hidden. A hidden region is not drawn; `in_region` and `lane_reaches` that name it skip. The Godot adapter hides a part in an instance by the game's nodes marked with metadata `part`, and records hidden regions on the root for the game's interface. No short command hides parts or regions in this stage (JSON Patch does).
- **Runtime file `isoblock-runtime/3`** (SPEC 13.7): `projection` (the screen vectors, so adapters need no trigonometry), the camera in its form, `base`, the hidden parts and regions, and every `x-` key.
- **Patches:** `move` takes `h±n`, `add` takes `u,v,h`, `lock` takes `pos.h`; `rot` keeps the base height; `remove` drops the object's `hideParts` entries; `compare` counts base heights in `moved`.
- **Editor:** both camera forms, heights, a drag that keeps the base height, the field `pos.h (<unit>)` with its lock, Duplicate keeping the base height, and hidden parts and regions in a state.
- **Fixtures in new files.** The tests of release 0.11.0 iterate `tests/golden/sort.json`, the runtime, export, Godot, editor and invalid case lists, so every stage 12 case is in a new file (`tests/golden/sort-levels.json`, `tests/fixtures/levels/`, `tests/fixtures/heights/`, `tests/fixtures/runtime3/`) and the suite of `main` passes on this branch. The `isoblock-runtime/2` files of `tests/fixtures/runtime/` go with release 0.12.0.
- Requests not taken into this stage are listed in SPEC 17 as asked for and not scheduled: a relation that puts an object on another, paths between levels, overhanging parts above a clear height, slice lengths per type, visibility and coverage reports, a height budget, generated road markings, pruning to a frame region, `sort_consistency` that does not flip under small moves, short commands for parts and regions.
