## Done

- **Adapter** (`adapters/godot/isoblock_runtime.gd`, 397 lines with comments):
  - `build(data, color_of, scenes)` returns `{ root, code, message, unmapped }` and builds `Objects` (meta and anchors only), `Sprites` (one `Node2D` per sprite with meta `object`, `key`, `slice`; child order = engine rule, ascending key and the sprite list on equal keys; every `z_index` 0, no y-sort), `Zones` and `Lanes`. Debug faces per piece in the object's flat color.
  - Instancing: a mapped type's sprite holds an instance at the pivot (projected footprint center at h = 0) with meta `object`, `type`, `rot`, `slice`; a sliced object holds one instance per slice under a `Polygon2D` hull mask with `clip_children` = `CLIP_CHILDREN_ONLY`. `unmapped` lists the missing types once, in the order of their first object, `[]` without `scenes`.
  - `apply_state(root, name)` (`""` = default, unknown = `E_STATE`, actors untouched).
  - Actors: `add_actor`, `move_actor`, `remove_actor` (`E_ACTOR`), placed after every scene sprite with a key at most theirs and in the order added among equal keys; the game's node or a box in `color`.
  - `sort_key(dir, footprint)` and `order_direction(camera)` in double precision (a `Vector2`/`Vector3` holds single precision); a `Vector2` position is read as the shortest decimal with the same single-precision value. `faces(camera, dir, box)` is public. `E_Z_RANGE` is removed; `check` also requires `states` and every object's `sprites`.
- **Adapter tests** (`adapters/godot/test/adapter_test.gd`, 343 checks on `walk`): projection golden vectors, the 15 keys of `tests/golden/sort.json` through `sort_key`, the tree and draw order (also with all keys equal, objects in either order), faces, `unmapped`, instances and masks (hull convex, made of projected corners, holding every corner), states, actors, actor order on equal keys (after the tied sprite, in the order added, kept across a move), the error codes, 4,098 parts in one object building fine.
- **Harness** (`scripts/godot/run.ts`, `actor.ts`, `measure.ts`, `adapters/godot/test/cross_check.gd`): every export case once and every case of `tests/fixtures/godot/cases.json` with its states in order; instanced types from white art colored through `modulate`; the actor along its path in the last state against the SVG of the scene with `isoblock-actor` added; `unmapped` compared; two runs byte-identical; each polygon clipped to the frame before the box of an object alone is taken; a GDScript `SCRIPT ERROR` in any Godot run is a failure.
- **Editor**: `searchesGrid`, the `defer` argument of `updateResults` and `rerunResults` (`src/core/incremental.ts`); the store keeps a `stale` set: during a drag `reachable` and `sort_consistency` keep their last result, and the drop or a cancel runs them again; the panel dims those rows and says `out of date: runs again when the drag ends`; `window.isoblock.state().stale`.
