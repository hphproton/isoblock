## Scope

Stage 15 is the one ROADMAP planned after stage 14, designed for any isometric game with paths that turn, slopes, stairs and seats that face something: a lane is a polyline in any direction with a height per point; every lane check, the relations that name lanes and `occluders` work on that shape; `reachable` crosses levels through the lanes that join them; the relation `facing`, reserved since stage 4, is measured; the solver may turn objects. It adds no command, one flag (`solve --rotate`) and optional scene keys, changes neither the runtime file nor the Godot adapter, and needs no new dependency.

## Decisions

- **Lane shape** (SPEC 9.8): one piece per segment, the segment widened by half the width on each side and not past its ends, with a surface that runs from one end height to the other. A lane of two points along u or v keeps its one rectangle, so every result stages 1 to 14 measured stays; what changes is that the lanes they skipped are measured. The handoff plan named discs at the bends; they would close the outer corner of a bend, but the editor and `render` draw each segment's rectangle, a disc needs circle geometry in every check, and the drawn and the measured lane would differ. SPEC 18 states the corner.
- **Measures on the shape:** overlap by the separating-axis penetration (for an axis-parallel rectangle the `no_overlap` rule), distance by the polygon distance of stage 9 (for two rectangles the `clearance` distance), and for `lane_apart` the largest overlap of one piece with another rather than the area of the union, which would need polygon unions with no gain for the check. `lane_clear` on a ramp compares an object's box with the lowest and highest surface under it; `lane_reaches` keeps every meeting with the edge inside the region.
- **Levels:** `reachable` with the new `toLevel` builds one grid per level and joins them through connector lanes, whose ends lie at two levels and that nothing blocks; the path length adds each connector's length in (u, v, h). Without `toLevel` nothing changes: `levels` has a stair lane between two levels, and its checks keep their results.
- **`facing`:** measured from a facing point (an object's footprint center or an anchor) along a `front` (`+u`, `-u`, `+v`, `-v`, turned by `rot`) to a target point (an object, the nearest point of a zone or of a lane); the violation is the excess angle in radians, so that it weighs like the distances in the soft penalty. `front` is a new optional key on types and anchors, because `facing` on anchors is a free string. An anchor may be `a` of `facing` only.
- **Turning:** `solve --rotate` may set `rot` on movable objects without rotation or position locks; distance is measured between footprint centers, and fewer turns win ties. Without the flag the solver is the one of stage 14, so the solver fixtures keep their results.
- **SPEC points from the stage 14 review:** the part rule of `hide` and `show` finds the part as anchors are found (the first split at which both the object and its part exist), so the parts of `a/b` can be named next to an object `a`, and the error names the longest object id; a schema detail that only says a value must match the `then` or `else` of a condition is left out, like those of `oneOf`.
- **Earlier fixtures:** six earlier expected files hold results of lanes that stage 14 skipped. Their new versions are in `tests/fixtures/paths/earlier/`, so that release 0.14.0 still passes its suite here; SPEC 17 says they replace the earlier files for this stage, and the release commit moves them over.
