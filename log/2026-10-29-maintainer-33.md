## Findings

1. **A turned object is not constrained when the search does not move it further.** `solveWithRotation` hands each assignment of rotations to the stage 14 solver as a new scene in which the turned object already stands at its turned footprint, so that solver treats it as an object that has not moved (no view or overlap constraint) and counts the overlaps of that scene as overlaps before the solve. SPEC 8: the constraints apply to the footprint at its new rotation, and two footprints may overlap only when they overlapped in the input. A counter (3 x 1, front `+v`) at [0, 2], a locked box (1 x 1) at [1.1, 0.6] and a locked stool at [8, 2.25], with a hard `facing` of the counter toward the stool (angle 30): `solve --rotate` reports `solved` with rot 270 at [1, 1], whose footprint overlaps the box by 0.6; the input has no overlap. The reference turns it and moves it to [0.1, 1].
2. **A turned object can leave the grid.** The turn keeps the footprint center, and when the search does not move the object again its changed coordinates need not be multiples of 0.05: a type of 1 x 0.75 at [2, 2] facing a locked stool ends at rot 270 and [2.125, 1.875]. The builder's own test helper `expectConstraints` rejects that proposal.
3. **The conflict set ignores `--rotate`.** `solveRotated` computes the minimal conflict set with the solver that does not turn. With hard relations, in this order, `inside` a slot that fits the counter only at rot 0 or 180, `facing` a long lane that it faces only after a quarter turn, and `clear_of` that lane with `min` 1, `solve --rotate` reports the set [c2], although the scene with `c2` alone is solved with `--rotate`. Deletion filtering with the solver that turns gives [c1, c2].
4. Minor: SPEC 10 asks for a short arrow along the front of a `facing` relation; the overlay draws a line without a head.

The three solver defects are outside the fixtures: `turn` keeps the counter on the grid and away from other objects, and `turn-locked` cannot turn. Everything else of the stage meets its rules.

Decision: not ready. As the owner chose, a builder session fixes findings 1 to 4 on this branch, with tests; the maintainer then reviews again and makes the release commit.
